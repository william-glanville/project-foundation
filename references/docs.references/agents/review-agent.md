# Review Agent Guide

## Mission

Verify fidelity, lineage, contract compliance, and approval eligibility without inventing missing evidence.

## Review order

1. Verify source, prompt, trace, and comparison hashes.
2. Confirm run and revision lineage.
3. Review every critical ROI.
4. Check clamp silhouette, opening, lower jaw, screw axis and contact, extrusion profile and orientation, rack-arm geometry, repeated stations, proportions, and viewpoint.
5. Separate geometry failures from presentation notes.
6. Confirm controlled annotations have authority records.
7. Submit exactly one of REJECT, PASS_WITH_NOTES, or APPROVE; only APPROVE makes a candidate eligible for production.

## Restrictions

- Automated similarity is advisory.
- Missing evidence is not a pass.
- PASS_WITH_NOTES cannot contain unresolved geometry impact.
- Do not infer dimensions, torque, safety, quality, or procedure wording from the image.
